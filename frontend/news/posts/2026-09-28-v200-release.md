# v2.0.0 - The Next Era of EFTForge

**Released:** 2026-09-28

Introducing EFTForge v2.0.0. The next era of Escape from Tarkov weapon build simulation, powered by Kitbash! Arcadia.

---

## Kitbash! Arcadia: The 3D Builder

![Kitbash! Arcadia](./news/images/v200/kitbashArcadia.png "Kitbash! Arcadia")

Kitbash! started out as **Kitbash! Sirius**, the 2D sprite compositor that has drawn every build image on EFTForge since v1.6.0. **Kitbash! Arcadia** is the next version: the same image renderer, plus a full 3D engine running right in your browser.

Switch the builder to 3D with the **2D / 3D** toggle at the top left, and it remembers your choice:

![3D Builder](./news/images/v200/3dBuilder.webp "3D Builder")

- **Mod on the model.** Every slot sits around the gun as a box. Left click one to pick a part, right click to empty it. Installs, removals and conflicts flash on the model, just like in 2D.
- **Two ways to pick parts.** Use the attachment table docked on the right, or turn on the **Compact Picker** for the in-game style modding dropdown, with trader availability badges that follow your trader levels.
- **Sight Picture.** Look through every sight on your build, cycle between them (V), switch scope modes (M) and zoom variable optics with the mouse wheel.

![Sight Picture Mode](./news/images/v200/sightPicture.webp "Sight Picture Mode")

- **Simulate ADS.** Start from the hip and aim in just like the game, with arm stamina, overswing, and weapon sway driven by your build's weight and ergonomics, your Strength and skill levels, and your equipment ergo. If you want to dig deeper, **Advanced Diagnostics** shows live graphs of the whole aiming model.

![Advanced Diagnostics](./news/images/v200/advDiag.webp "Advanced Diagnostics")

- **Range.** Set the target distance and aspect ratio. You can also hide the range and put a flat backdrop, a chroma key green or blue, or any custom color behind your gun.
- **Tactical Devices.** Switch lights and lasers (visible and IR) on and see their beams.
- **Muzzle Smoke & Flash.** Fire one shot, a burst, or hold the trigger at your weapon's rate of fire, and compare the smoke and flash each muzzle device makes: smoke per shot, how much of your view around the aim point it hides, and where the flame and sparks go.

![Muzzle Smoke & Flash Simulation](./news/images/v200/smokeFlashSim.webp "Muzzle Smoke & Flash Simulation")

- **Loaded rounds.** With **Assume Full Magazine** on, the magazines and chamber hold the ammo you selected.

**Good to know:**

- The 3D builder renders in real time on your GPU. Using it while Escape from Tarkov is running is **not recommended**, as it may affect your in-game performance. The **2D / 3D** toggle at the top left switches between it and the classic 2D workbench anytime, and switching to 2D shuts the 3D view down completely, so it costs nothing in the background.
- Mobile keeps the 2D workbench.
- In the desktop app, the 3D builder works in **Connected to EFTForge.com** mode. **Fully local** mode stays 2D, so nothing leaves your computer.

### Something Looks Off in 3D?

Same deal as Kitbash! Sirius: a part in the wrong spot, a missing model, a sight that doesn't line up, anything at all, report it to me on [Bilibili](https://space.bilibili.com/650421245) or [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/SouthHorizons76/EFTForge/issues). A screenshot of the issue helps a lot!

## TrueErgo Replaces EvoErgo

EvoErgo estimated overswing with a fitted curve. **TrueErgo** uses the exact rule the game uses: aiming in overswings once weight x (1 - ergonomics / 100) passes 3 kg.

- **TrueErgoDelta (TED)** is how much ergo your build could lose before it overswings, in plain ergo points. Above 0 you're fine, below 0 you overswing.
- **OverSwing** is now exact. It shares a row with the new **overswing percentage**: 0% until 3 kg effective weight, full sway at 7 kg effective weight or above, so you'll see something like "YES - 45%".
- **Arm stamina** now uses the game's own drain formula, including 0.4% less drain per Strength level.
- **MOA** now factors in the loaded round's accuracy modifier.
- In the optimizer, **Use EvoErgo** is now **Use TrueErgo**, and the Max MOA constraint accounts for your loaded round too. Your saved optimizer settings carry over.
- Community build cards show TED and can be sorted by **Highest TED**. Builds published before this update work it out from their saved ergo and weight.

TrueErgo grew out of the EvoErgo concept by **SpaceMonkey37**. Thanks again for pioneering the idea of judging ergonomics together with weight!

## Optimizer: Include Unpriced Parts

There's a new **Include unpriced parts** toggle in the optimizer's market settings, on by default. Parts you can't buy from traders or the flea market with your current access (Arena only, barter only, flea banned) can now make it into your build.

Their price is unknown, so they never count as free: a priced part with the same stats always wins, a budget limit leaves them out, and they stay off the price curves. They show "-" in the parts list and aren't added to the totals.

## Loudness Stat

Muzzle devices, suppressors and a few barrels now show their **Loudness** as a sortable column in the attachment table and the optimizer's parts list, and the advanced stats panel adds it up for your build.

Heads up: the game never actually reads this number. Only whether a suppressor is installed changes the gunshot sound and how far away bots can hear you. lol

## Stat Tracker: Full History

The Stat Tracker still opens on the last 7 days, but the new date picker next to the search box lists every past sync day with changes, so you can look back as far as the history goes.
